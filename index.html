REFERENCE / BEST PRACTICE

UTM field guide

Use consistent values so campaign reports remain readable.

04 / 04
01

utm_source

Identifies the platform or publisher sending traffic.

google · instagram · linkedin · newsletter
02

utm_medium

Describes the marketing channel or traffic type.

cpc · paid_social · email · organic_social
03

utm_campaign

Names the promotion, initiative or campaign.

diwali_solar_offer · q4_launch · webinar_2026
04

utm_content

Distinguishes creatives, placements or calls to action.

hero_banner · carousel_2 · footer_cta
05

utm_term

Commonly stores paid-search keywords or audience terms.

solar_panel_price · rooftop_installation
06

Keep naming consistent

Choose one case style and delimiter. Lowercase snake_case is a useful convention, not a platform requirement.

lowercase · underscores · no spaces

Important measurement notes

  • UTM values are case-sensitive in many analytics reporting contexts. Instagram and instagram can split reporting.
  • Do not add UTMs to internal links on your own website; that can overwrite attribution.
  • Avoid putting personal, confidential or sensitive information in query parameters. URLs may be visible in history, logs and referrer data.
  • Use the same source/medium vocabulary across campaigns and document your team's conventions.
